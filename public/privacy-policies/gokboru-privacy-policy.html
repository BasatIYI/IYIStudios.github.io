Gökbörü Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Our Privacy Policy was last updated on 08.11.2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of Your information when You play Gökbörü (the "Game") distributed via Steam (Valve Corporation) and related platforms, and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

Interpretation and Definitions

Definitions

  • Application / Game: refers to Gökbörü, the 3D territory-capture action roguelite developed and published by IYI Studios.
  • Company: (referred to as either "the Company", "We", "Us" or "Our") refers to IYI Studios, Türkiye.
  • Platform: refers to Steam, owned and operated by Valve Corporation.
  • Personal Data: is any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Service: refers to the Game and related digital services.
  • You: means the individual player accessing or playing Gökbörü.

Information Collection and Data Handling

No Direct Account Registration

Gökbörü does not require You to create an independent user account with IYI Studios. We do not collect or store Your real name, physical address, email address, or payment credentials directly on our servers.

Steamworks Platform Integration

When You play Gökbörü on Steam, the Game interacts with Valve's Steamworks API to provide essential gaming features:

  • Steam ID: Used locally and via Steamworks to associate game progress, achievements, and Steam Leaderboard rankings with Your Steam profile.
  • Steam Cloud Saves: Game save data (unlocked boons, story progress, high scores, settings) is stored locally on Your device and synchronized with Valve's Steam Cloud servers under Valve's Privacy Policy.
  • Steam Achievements & Stats: High scores, completed runs, and boss defeat milestones are reported directly to Steamworks.

Diagnostic and Crash Data

In the event of an unexpected software crash or technical failure, non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as operating system version, CPU/GPU hardware specifications, and crash stack traces) may be generated locally or processed through Steam's crash reporting infrastructure to help us diagnose and fix bugs.

No Third-Party Advertising or Tracking SDKs

Gökbörü contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no behavioral user tracking, and no monetization trackers. We do not sell, rent, or monetize player data in any form.

Use of Data

Any data processed by Gökbörü is used strictly to:

  • Deliver and maintain gameplay functionality, settings, and progress.
  • Support Steam features (Steam Cloud, Steam Achievements, Leaderboards).
  • Identify and patch software defects and optimize performance.

Children's Privacy

Gökbörü does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Since no personal data is collected directly, our game is compliant with COPPA and European privacy standards.

Governing Law & Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Türkiye, without regard to its conflict of law provisions.
